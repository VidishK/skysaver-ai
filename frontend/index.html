Plan once

Plan once. Travel without thinking.

Add your flights, stays, ground transport, and meetings. SkySaver holds the whole shape of your trip in mind — and knows what depends on what.

  • One intake captures preferences once — seat class, layover tolerance, hotel tier.
  • Every leg is connected — change one and SkySaver re-checks the rest.
  • No spreadsheets. No 11 browser tabs. One coherent itinerary.
When things break

When something breaks, you'll already have an answer.

SkySaver doesn't just alert you. It ranks alternatives against your stored preferences and shows the cascade — exactly what each option does to your hotel, your meeting, your connections.

  • Best-for-you ranking, not just cheapest or fastest.
  • Cascade preview before you commit a single dollar.
  • Severity badges: low risk, tight buffer, shifts hotel — at a glance.
Under the hood

Powered by Gemini. Watched by MongoDB MCP.

Gemini 2.5 Flash reads your booking PDFs, reasons over your trip, and answers your questions. The MongoDB MCP server keeps every leg coherent in real time.

  • Upload a confirmation — Gemini Vision OCR fills the form for you.
  • Ask anything in plain language — it reads your live trip to answer.
  • Polls every 10 seconds. You'll never be the last to know.
